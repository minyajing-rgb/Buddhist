# Harvard Divinity School / Harvard Method Notes

## 1. Research should span an information landscape

Harvard Divinity School Library explicitly points researchers across media, texts, data, maps, images, archival materials, specialist databases, and special collections. A reusable atlas should therefore model source *types*, not just websites.

Official anchors:
- https://library.hds.harvard.edu/
- https://library.hds.harvard.edu/research/research-guides
- https://library.hds.harvard.edu/key-databases
- https://library.hds.harvard.edu/research/mdiv-senior-paper-faq

## 2. Buddhist Studies discovery stack

HDS Buddhist Studies resources include:
- ATLA Religion Database;
- Bibliography of Asian Studies;
- BUDA/BDRC;
- Digital Dictionary of Buddhism;
- scholarly encyclopedias and Oxford bibliographies/reference works.

Official anchor:
- https://library.hds.harvard.edu/key-databases/buddhist-studies

Reuse pattern for another discipline:
- discipline index;
- area-studies index;
- object/corpus database;
- terminology dictionary;
- reference encyclopedia;
- annotated bibliography.

## 3. Religious literacy / contextual study

Harvard Religion and Public Life emphasizes:
- internal diversity;
- change over time;
- embeddedness in culture;
- context and power;
- nonsectarian analytical framing.

Official anchors:
- https://rpl.hds.harvard.edu/what-we-do/our-approach
- https://rpl.hds.harvard.edu/religion-context
- https://rpl.hds.harvard.edu/what-we-do/our-approach/core-principles

## 4. Research workflow / bibliography

HDS Library support includes:
- research strategy;
- finding relevant sources;
- building exhaustive bibliography;
- citation management;
- obtaining materials worldwide.

Official anchors:
- https://library.hds.harvard.edu/services
- https://library.hds.harvard.edu/research/mdiv-senior-paper-faq

## 5. Primary sources

Harvard Library defines primary sources by their evidentiary relationship to the question, not by whether they are handwritten, printed, digitized, or microfilmed.

Official anchor:
- https://guides.library.harvard.edu/HistSciInfo/primary

## 6. Qualitative research

Harvard Library explicitly supports:
- interviews;
- field research;
- oral histories;
- participant observation;
- questionnaires/focus groups;
- social media interactions.

Official anchor:
- https://library.harvard.edu/services-tools/qualitative-research-support

## 7. Digital scholarship

Harvard Library supports:
- OCR / HTR;
- NLP;
- collections-as-data;
- immersive visualization;
- 3D scanning / photogrammetry-related workflows;
- mapping/GIS;
- digital publication.

Official anchors:
- https://library.harvard.edu/how-to/digital-scholarship-program
- https://library.harvard.edu/how-to/manage-and-share-your-data
- https://library.harvard.edu/services-tools/harvard-library-apis-datasets

## 8. Generalization beyond religion

The reusable pattern is:

```
Reference overview
→ specialist databases
→ primary evidence
→ archives/objects
→ qualitative/community evidence
→ computation
→ bibliography
→ public interpretation
→ QA
```
