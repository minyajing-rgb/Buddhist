# OFFLINE ACCESS GUIDE｜线上找不到时，去哪里、怎么找

## 原则
**先目录，后现场。** 不要直接飞去图书馆。

### 统一操作
1. 在线检索并记录 **shelfmark / call number / item ID**。
2. 检查是否有 digitized image / IIIF / PDF / microfilm。
3. 检查读者资格、预约和复制规则。
4. 邮件中提供：题名、编号、语言、年代、你需要查看的页/叶、用途。
5. 只有在“没有数字影像 / 需要物质形态 / 需核对未公开档案”时现场访问。

## 第一层：全球大馆
- **British Library (London)** — Gandhāra, Stein/Dunhuang, Central Asian, Pāli/Asian collections.
- **BnF (Paris)** — Pelliot Dunhuang, Chinese/Tibetan/Sanskrit/Central Asian manuscripts.
- **Library of Congress (Washington, DC)** — Tibetan collections, Gandhara scroll, Asian rare materials.
- **Cambridge University Library** — Buddhist Sanskrit manuscripts; substantial digitization.
- **Harvard-Yenching** — East Asian + Tibetan, 17th/18th c. Kanjur/Tanjur editions.
- **Bodleian** — Sanskrit/Tibetan/Asian manuscripts and early printed materials.

## 第二层：国家级本土馆
- **National Library of China** — Dunhuang manuscripts, Zhaocheng Jin Canon, rare Buddhist books.
- **National Central Library (Taiwan)** — Chinese rare books and Buddhist materials.
- **National Diet Library (Japan)** — Japanese printed/rare Buddhist materials; remote photoduplication and digital transmission services.
- **National Archives of Nepal / NGMCP** — Nepalese Sanskrit/Newar/Tibetan manuscript microfilms and catalog.
- **National Library of Sri Lanka** — Buddhist palm-leaf manuscripts in Sinhala/Pāli/Sanskrit.
- **National Library of Thailand** — palm leaves, inscriptions, Thai folding books; manuscript database + D-Library.
- **National Library of Myanmar** — Pāli/Myanmar/Mon palm-leaf and Buddhist digital collections.
- **National Library of Mongolia** — Tibetan/Mongolian Buddhist holdings and BDRC-linked digitization.

## 第三层：特殊实体
- **Haeinsa, Korea** — Tripitaka Koreana woodblocks; material heritage witness rather than a normal circulating book collection.
- **Monastic / private collections** — often require local project catalogues, BDRC/NGMCP/field-survey mediation, or institution-specific permission.

## 什么时候必须现场看？
- 影像分辨率不够；
- 需要纸张/贝叶/墨色/装帧/补写痕迹；
- 需要检查叶序和缺叶；
- 目录年代与实物判断冲突；
- 需要未公开档案、旧卡片目录或馆员协助；
- 研究问题本身就是 codicology / paleography / provenance。

## 数据库要求
每个实体馆藏记录必须至少有：
- institution
- city/country
- strength
- online-first path
- onsite trigger
- access steps
- official URL
